# Regulated hospital operations

Read only the applicable references:

- [Pharmacy and supply](../../../docs/domain/india/pharmacy-and-supply.md),
  [PharmaConnect](../../../docs/api/domains/pharmacy.md) and
  [SupplyConnect](../../../docs/api/domains/supply.md).
- [Facility and quality](../../../docs/domain/india/facility-and-quality.md) for
  licences, assets/readiness, cleaning, incidents, waste and continuity.
- [Source register](../../../docs/domain/india/sources.md) for source status,
  current-policy verification and accountable applicability review.

Establish the verified tenant and narrower outlet/premise/facility scope, service
and jurisdiction, current actor grant, policy/version and actual licence or
certificate scope/validity. Submitted evidence, broad organizational ownership,
historic verification and an expiry reminder are not current operating authority.

For medicine/stock work, distinguish formulation/strength/pack from batch/expiry,
and quarantined/released/reserved/blocked stock from physical totals. Retain exact
units and source/destination posting owners through receipt, discrepancy, return
and disposal. FEFO does not override safety restrictions. Verify current drug
schedule, price-order and applicable controlled-drug policy; a static old list or
one register retention period is not a complete regulatory rule set.

For facilities, distinguish licence, accreditation, empanelment, staff privilege,
task completion and actual readiness. Preserve accountable physical-action
evidence. A maintenance or cleaning task for an old location/version cannot
release another bed; a waste manifest is not proof of treatment/disposal.

Select relevant `IN-PH`/`IN-FQ` acceptance cases: cross-premise/outlet access,
revoked authority, stale versions, partial receipt, duplicate posting, incorrect
unit conversion, unsafe/expired stock, altered evidence, expired scope, wrong-room
completion, pending legal hold, physical handoff discrepancy and downtime recovery.

Use current issuer text and qualified pharmacy/quality/facility/legal reviewers
for consequential decisions. Do not equate a software feature with accreditation
or a licence, invent mandatory nationwide rules, or autonomously activate a policy.
No real dispensing, destruction, filing, credential change or production action
is authorized by invoking this skill.
