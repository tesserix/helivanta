# Staff and RBAC research and implementation evidence

Research checked 2026-11-06. Companion to the [design](README.md). Primary sources inform the design; they do not select a customer's applicable law or prove that Helivanta implements a control. Findings below are paraphrased. No real staff/patient records were inspected.

### Research synthesis

The external evidence supports role bundles, conditional resource scope, dated local authority, lifecycle review and separate administrative/technical controls. **The hospital/independent tree, readable profile prefixes, typed independent Workforce scope, intake default and outsourced-service design are Helivanta proposals**, derived from the user's requirements and existing ADRs. No source mandates those exact identifiers or a particular commercial organization tree.

This is a focused primary-source review, not an exhaustive survey of commercial EHR internals. No undocumented Epic/Cerner implementation is asserted. No paid standard's full text was reviewed. Legal/regulatory conclusions require current jurisdiction-specific review; registration, licence, employment and local clinical authority remain different facts.

## Main-branch reconciliation

Checked before committing against `origin/main` at `ab9f8a653fa0c238f7824380f285bb8490832e67` on 2026-11-06:

- The organization access decision is now ADR-0044, not the local worktree’s earlier ADR number. All links in this package use its current path. The single-owner approval wording remains a review question.
- [ADR-0051](../../adr/0051-shared-department-owner-boundary.md) records the shared Organization owner; [Organization API](../../api/organization.md) and `backend/internal/modules/organization/` exist. The historical “no department entity” observation is superseded.
- [ADR-0052](../../adr/0052-staff-source-command-authorization.md) and [ADR-0055](../../adr/0055-department-scoped-assignments.md) define guarded commands and department scope. Current `assignment_authority.go` selects both hospital and department candidates, uses current membership/scope epochs and rechecks after the graph decision. It is not leave-only or hospital-only as in the older snapshot.
- `contracts/staff-access.json` now includes optional department IDs and multiple versioned Workforce and pharmacy-binding templates, including `workforce.department_posting_reviewer.v1`. Reuse these contracts; do not narrow them to the historical single-template description.
- `backend/internal/modules/pharmacy/staff_department_bindings*.go` and [Workforce department links](../../api/workforce-department-links.md) exist. Review their exact completion/activation evidence before extending them; the presence of these files is not proof of independent-employer HR or a live production rollout.
- The original `scoped_leave_review.go` path no longer exists; the historical table links the current leave-review-scope implementation instead. It does not assert identical source content across revisions.
- The current reconciler still appends `RoleTenantAdmin` to declared grants, and independent Workforce employer scope remains a proposed extension in this package.
- ADR-0055 covers a department’s current descendants and explicitly rejects exact-node-only department access. The draft preserves it; proposed fixed/reviewed outlet sets concern product scopes, not a change to department semantics.

This is a targeted reconciliation of the document’s affected references and major current-state claims, not a second end-to-end implementation audit.
