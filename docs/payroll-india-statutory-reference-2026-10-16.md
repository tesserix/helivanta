# Indian payroll statutory landscape — design reference (as of 2026-10-16)

Purpose: shape the payroll configuration model (#1041) and payroll runs (#1040). This is **not** a source of values. Nothing here is seeded into any tenant. A hospital enters each value with its own legal citation, and HR/legal approve it. Every figure below is from secondary sources (payroll vendors, law firms) and must be checked against the Gazette before use.

## What the landscape means for the design

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Design consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The EPF wage ceiling went from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 **on 17 September 2026** (S.O. 5109(E)), so September contributions are split at that date.                                                                                                                                                        | Statutory parameters take effect on a **day**, not a month. A payroll period can span two revisions, and the run must apportion them.                                                                                                                   |
| Under the four Labour Codes (in force 21 Nov 2025), "wages" are basic + DA + retaining allowance. Listed exclusions (HRA, conveyance, overtime, commission, employer PF, statutory bonus…) above 50% of total remuneration are added back to wages.                                                  | Every salary head declares a **wage class**: `wages`, `excluded` (counts toward the 50% test) or `outside` (not remuneration, e.g. reimbursements). The calculator reports Code wages, the add-back and the 50% test result.                            |
| PF, ESI, gratuity and bonus all compute on Code wages, not on gross pay or basic.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Statutory rules (slice 2) consume Code wages from the calculator. They never read a head named "Basic".                                                                                                                                                 |
| The ESI ceiling is still ₹21,000 gross/month (₹25,000 for persons with disability); rates are 0.75% employee and 3.25% employer. A raise is under discussion. An employee who crosses the ceiling mid contribution-period stays covered until it ends (8 May 2026 notification).                     | ESI needs contribution periods (Apr–Sep and Oct–Mar) and coverage that sticks for the period. A plain monthly threshold is not enough. Ceilings and rates are tenant entries.                                                                           |
| Professional tax is levied by states and capped at ₹2,500 a year (Art. 276). The month can matter: Maharashtra and Karnataka charge ₹300 in February. Some states slab by gender (Maharashtra) or half-year (Kerala). Over a dozen states and UTs levy none, and Odisha repealed its PT Act in 2026. | PT rule sets are per **state**, with month-specific amounts, optional gender slabs, and a monthly, half-yearly or annual period. A hospital's establishment state picks the rule set. "No PT" is an explicit choice, never a fallback.                  |
| The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026: "tax year" replaces assessment year, salary TDS moves from s.192 to **s.392**, and Form 16 becomes Form 130 (sources disagree on whether Form 24Q becomes 138 or 143).                                                             | Tax rule sets are keyed by **tax year** and **regime**, and cite the section of the governing Act. Form and section numbers are data, not code.                                                                                                         |
| New regime FY 2026-27 (secondary sources): slabs at ₹4/8/12/16/20/24 lakh rising from 0% to 30% in 5% steps, ₹75,000 standard deduction, rebate up to ₹60,000 for income ≤ ₹12 lakh with marginal relief, 4% cess, surcharge capped at 25%. The old regime remains elective.                         | Tax configuration needs ordered slabs, a standard deduction, a rebate with **marginal relief**, surcharge bands with their own marginal relief, and cess. Each employee elects a regime for the tax year. All of these are tenant entries per tax year. |
| Gratuity: 15/26 × last drawn wages × completed years; fixed-term employees qualify after **1 year** (permanent employees after 5); part years over six months round up; ₹20 lakh ceiling.                                                                                                            | Gratuity is an accrual and final-settlement rule (later slice). It uses Code wages and needs employment type and service history from enrollment.                                                                                                       |
| Statutory bonus (Code on Wages s.26, S.O. 4710/4711(E), 25 Aug 2026): eligible at ≤ ₹21,000/month wages; computed on the greater of ₹7,000 and the applicable minimum wage; 8.33%–20%; at least 30 days worked; establishments with 20+ employees.                                                   | Annual bonus is a separate rule set, applied to an accounting year (later slice).                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Delivery slices for #1041

1. **Salary heads, salary structures, deterministic calculator and preview** (this slice). Covers heads for earnings, deductions and employer contributions: fixed or percentage bases, dependency graph, proration, minimum payable days, month applicability, rounding order, caps, taxability, wage class, payslip label and order. Also versioned structures scoped by department, designation and grade, and a synthetic preview.
2. **Statutory rule sets with maker–checker approval**: PF/EPS/EDLI, ESI, PT by state, LWF by state. Day-effective, cited, approved by a second person.
3. **Income-tax rule sets and regime election**: slabs, deductions, rebate with marginal relief, surcharge, cess, per-employee regime and declarations (Form 124 data). Regime election, claim heads (5a) and declarations with encrypted landlord and lender PANs (5b) are delivered; declaration evidence (5b-ii) and the TDS projection (6) remain.
4. **Employee salary assignment and payslip configuration**: CTC and structure per staff member, private bank/PAN/UAN fields behind their own permission, payslip formats, field sequencing, YTD, and HR settings switches.

Bonus and gratuity rule sets go with payroll processing (#1040), which consumes the finalised attendance snapshot (#1039).
