# Sidebar Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Stable grouped zone order, no partial rail while permissions load, collapse as a foot-of-rail icon.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-sidebar-design.md` (#1270)

## Task 1: Zone order and groups

- `packages/ui/src/zones.ts`: `Zone.group: "overview" | "care" | "operations" | "admin"`. `ZONES` reordered per D1.
- Test `zones.test.ts`: `ZONES.map(z => z.key)` equals `["dashboard","patients","medicore","lab","pharmacy","emergency","supply","workforce","settings"]`. Each group's zones are contiguous.

## Task 2: Rail rendering

- `hms-shell.tsx`:
  - the main nav renders non-admin zones, with a divider between groups;
  - the foot of the rail holds the admin zones, then the collapse button (icon only).
- `usePermissions().isLoading` makes the rail and panel render placeholders (`data-testid="nav-placeholder"`, `aria-hidden`) with `aria-busy` on the nav, and the zone comes from `activeZone(pathname)`.
- Tests in `hms-shell.test.tsx`:
  - the rail lists zones in D1 order, with Settings after the zone nav;
  - while `/iam/me/permissions` is pending (no cache), there are no zone links, there are placeholders, and the heading is "Patients" on `/patients` (a single-page zone is headed by its name), not "Dashboard";
  - the collapse button sits outside the Zones nav and has no visible text.

## Task 3: Phone overlays (spec D4, D5)

- `hms-shell.tsx`: unpersisted `pagesOverlayOpen`. Below `md` the header button shows for every multi-page zone and opens the panel as an overlay with a scrim. Choosing a zone or page closes both overlays.
- `styles.css`: the stored-closed panel width applies from `md` only.
- Test `e2e/tests/phone-navigation.spec.ts` (390px): a Staff page opens from the header and the overlay closes; the expanded rail closes once a zone is chosen.
