# Deposits Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Take deposits at the counter and apply them against the final bill (#199 slice 1).

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-deposits-design.md

## Global Constraints

- Migration `0006_billing`; receipts gain `purpose` (`payment`, `deposit`).
- `409` codes: `account_closed`, `nothing_to_apply`, `no_final_bill`, `deposit_applied`.

## Review Focus

- Applying twice at once: never more than held.
- Voiding a deposit after it was applied.
- A payment after an application: capped at the new balance.
- A bill's paid/deposit figures supplied by a client: overwritten.
- Reader without the grant: 404.

### Task 1: Backend

`deposits.sql`, `deposits.go`, `deposits_test.go`; `cash.go` (purpose), `accounts.go`
(totals), `interim_bills.go` (bill figures), `module.go`.

### Task 2: Frontend

Take deposit and apply deposits in `cash-payments.tsx`; final bill figures in
`admission-bills.tsx`; Vitest.

### Task 3: Docs

`docs/api/domains/billing.md`, portfolio, reference.
